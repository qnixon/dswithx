The augmented matrix is an array of row arrays, so partial pivoting swaps
two rows by swapping the two array references in `m`, without copying any
numbers. The elimination and back substitution are `for` loops that update
the entries in place with `<-`.

OCaml's `**` takes two floats; writing `2.0 ** 3` is a compile error. A
small `power` helper converts the exponent with `float_of_int`. Speeds are
mapped onto −1 to 1 using the training range only, and every fit and error
is computed from the drives' (z, litres) pairs. No solver or fitting
function is called.
